### What makes a strong project?

For each published project, aim to include:

1. **Objective and scope** — what you're testing or investigating, and where.
2. **Environment and tools** — versions, setup, and assumptions.
3. **Method** — reproducible steps, queries, commands, or configuration.
4. **Evidence** — sanitized screenshots, sample logs, outputs, or test cases.
5. **Findings** — what the evidence shows, what it does not show, and any limitations.
6. **Remediation or next steps** — practical improvements and what you learned.
